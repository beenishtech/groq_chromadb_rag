Comprehensive Company Customer Refund and Return Policy:
At our company, customer satisfaction is our top priority. We want to ensure you are completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not entirely happy, we are here to help.

1. Eligibility for Refunds:
- Customers are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of the original purchase date.
- To qualify for a return, the item must be unused, in the same condition that you received it, and must be in its original packaging.
- Proof of purchase, such as an order confirmation email or receipt, is strictly required for all returns.

2. Non-Refundable Items:
- Gift cards, downloadable digital products, and software licenses cannot be returned or refunded.
- Items marked as final sale or clearance sales are also strictly non-refundable.

3. Refund Process:
- To initiate a refund, please contact our customer support team at support@company.com with your order number and reason for return.
- Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will notify you via email regarding the approval or rejection of your refund.
- Approved refunds will be processed automatically, and a credit will be applied to your original method of payment within 5 to 7 business days.
