Corporate IT Security Guidelines and Best Practices Manual:
Protecting company data, intellectual property, and internal networks is a critical responsibility for all employees. Adherence to these security protocols is mandatory across all departments.

1. Password Management and Credentials:
- All employees must use the corporate-approved enterprise password manager to generate and store secure credentials.
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and must include a secure mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special symbols.
- Reusing old passwords is strictly prohibited, and passwords must be updated every 90 days.

2. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) via an authenticator app or hardware token must be enabled on all corporate email accounts, cloud storage drives, VPNs, and version control systems (like GitHub or GitLab).

3. Device and Software Security:
- Operating systems, web browsers, and corporate antivirus solutions must be updated to their latest versions weekly. Automatic background updates should never be turned off.
- Company laptops must never be left unattended in public spaces and must be locked immediately whenever you step away from your desk.

4. Phishing and Social Engineering Awareness:
- Exercise extreme caution when opening unsolicited emails, unexpected messages, or clicking external links. Always verify the sender's email address carefully.
- Never download unverified attachments or share login credentials over phone, chat, or social media. Report any suspected phishing attempts or security anomalies immediately to the IT security department at security@company.com.
