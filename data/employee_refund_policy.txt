Employee Business Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy:
This policy outlines the guidelines and procedures for employees seeking reimbursement for expenses incurred while conducting official company business.

1. General Guidelines:
- All business travel and major expenses must be pre-approved by the department manager before booking.
- Employees are expected to exercise reasonable judgment when spending company funds.

2. Travel and Accommodation Limits:
- Hotel accommodations are strictly capped at $150 per night for standard rooms. Luxury or boutique hotels exceeding this limit will not be fully reimbursed unless approved in advance.
- Meal allowances are limited to a maximum of $40 per day during domestic travel. Alcohol expenses are not reimbursable under any circumstances.
- Economy class must be booked for all flights. Upgrades to business or first class are the employee's personal responsibility.

3. Submission Deadlines and Documentation:
- All expense reports and original itemized receipts must be submitted through the internal HR and finance portal within 7 days of returning from the business trip.
- Any expense submitted without valid receipts or after the 7-day deadline will be automatically rejected and will not be reimbursed by the company.
